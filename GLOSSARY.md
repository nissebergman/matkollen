# Matapp

A household tool that gathers the current week's supermarket Offers in one filterable view.

## Language

**Offer**:
A promotional price set by a Store on a product for a limited period. Member-only prices count as ordinary Offers. The household is assumed to be a member of every Chain.
_Avoid_: Deal, discount, campaign, erbjudande

**Offer Week**:
The period when an Offer is valid, normally one calendar week.
_Avoid_: Campaign period

**Chain**:
A supermarket brand whose Stores publish Offers: ICA, Coop or Hemköp.
_Avoid_: Retailer, brand

**Store**:
A single physical supermarket that belongs to a Chain.
_Avoid_: Shop, butik

**Location**:
A town or city whose Stores are covered. Linköping is the only Location for now, and more can be added later.
_Avoid_: Region, area
